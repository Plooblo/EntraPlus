    Is EntraPlus free?

    Yes, to look things up. The Free plan is read-only with no time limit: look up users, groups, devices and licences, and export lists to CSV. Making changes needs Pro, at £20 a month or £240 a year per technician, or 5 technicians for £1,000 a year. Every new organisation gets a free 30-day Pro trial for up to 5 technicians, with no card needed.

    How does the free trial work?

    The first person from your school to sign in sets up your organisation and becomes its manager. Your organisation then gets 30 days of Pro for up to 5 technicians, with no card needed. The manager hands out the seats. Each organisation gets one trial. When it ends, the app carries on as the read-only Free plan until you choose a plan.

    Does my organisation's data go through your servers?

    No. The app runs on your PC and talks directly to Microsoft and your own domain controllers. Nothing about your users, devices or policies is sent to us. Your EntraPlus account only holds your name, email and subscription.

    What permissions does it need?

    You sign in with your own admin account, and EntraPlus can never do more than your Entra role allows. User Administrator covers users and groups, Intune Administrator covers devices. A Global Administrator approves the app's permissions once for the organisation. Extra permissions for Pro device and policy features are only requested the first time you use them.

    Does it work with on-premises Active Directory?

    Yes, with Pro, including from cloud-only laptops that aren't joined to the domain. EntraPlus connects to your domain controller over the network or VPN, detects accounts that sync from AD and saves their changes there.

    Do I need to install PowerShell modules?

    Not for users, groups, devices or policies. Those talk to Microsoft Graph directly. Exchange and Active Directory features need a module each, and EntraPlus installs them for you with one click.

    Can I cancel?

    Any time from your account page. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then the app carries on as the read-only Free plan.

    Is it only for schools?

    It was built in a school, so the language and defaults suit schools and trusts: academic-year expiry dates, education licences, staff and student groups. It works the same for any organisation on Microsoft 365.

    Is EntraPlus made by Microsoft?

    No. EntraPlus is an independent product and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.
