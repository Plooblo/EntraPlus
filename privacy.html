Privacy and cookies

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starting template; have it checked before you take payments.

The EntraPlus app

The app runs on your computer and talks directly to Microsoft and your own domain controllers. Information about your users, devices, groups and policies is never sent to us. The app stores its settings, sign-in cache and activity log on your computer only.

Your EntraPlus account

When you create an account we store your name, organisation, email address and subscription status, so we can take payment and give you your licence key. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card details.

Analytics cookies

If you allow it, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It isn't loaded unless you choose Allow analytics. You can change your mind at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@entraplus.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
