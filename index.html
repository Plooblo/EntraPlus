    Safe by design

    Built not to break things

    Busy technicians need guard rails. These are built in.

    Your tenant stays yours

    • The app runs on your PC and talks directly to Microsoft and your own domain controllers.
    • You sign in as yourself, so it can never do more than your admin role allows.
    • Extra permissions are only asked for the first time you use the feature that needs them.
    • The activity log and your settings stay on your PC.

    Hard to break things

    • Deleting a user or group, or retiring or wiping a device, means typing its name first.
    • Live status is re-checked before you act, so you can't flip someone the wrong way.
    • Conditional Access templates start in report-only mode, with your own account excluded.
    • Every change is logged with who made it. Passwords, BitLocker keys and LAPS passwords never are.
    • The Free plan can't change anything at all.

    Plans and trial

    Is EntraPlus free?

    Yes, to look things up. The Free plan is read-only with no time limit: look up users, groups, devices and licences, and export lists to CSV. Making changes needs Pro, at £20 a month or £240 a year per technician, or 5 technicians for £1,000 a year. Every new organisation gets a free 30-day Pro trial for up to 5 technicians, with no card needed.

    How does the free trial work?

    The first person from your school to sign in sets up your organisation and becomes its manager. Your organisation then gets 30 days of Pro for up to 5 technicians, with no card needed. The manager hands out the seats. Each organisation gets one trial.

    What happens when the trial ends?

    The app carries on as the read-only Free plan until you choose a plan. Nothing in your tenant is changed or removed.

    How do technicians get Pro?

    Your manager buys seats on the EntraPlus website and gives one to each technician under People. Pro switches on the next time that technician signs in to the app.

    Can I cancel?

    Any time from your account page. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then the app carries on as the read-only Free plan.

    Security and data

    Does my organisation's data go through your servers?

    No. The app runs on your PC and talks directly to Microsoft and your own domain controllers. Nothing about your users, devices or policies is sent to us. Your EntraPlus account only holds your name, email and subscription.

    Is it safe to try on our live tenant?

    Yes. The Free plan is read-only, so nothing in your tenant can be changed. With Pro, risky actions are hidden in basic mode, and deleting or wiping needs the name typed first.

    What permissions does it need?

    You sign in with your own admin account, and EntraPlus can never do more than your Entra role allows. User Administrator covers users and groups, Intune Administrator covers devices. A Global Administrator approves the app's permissions once for the organisation. Extra permissions for Pro device and policy features are only requested the first time you use them.

    Can I control what each technician can do?

    Yes. Managers choose each person's role. Technicians use basic mode, which hides deleting, wiping and the leaver. Senior technicians can switch on advanced mode.

    Is EntraPlus made by Microsoft?

    No. EntraPlus is an independent product and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

    Setup

    Do we need a server?

    No. EntraPlus is a Windows app on your PC. For Active Directory features, the PC needs to reach a domain controller over your network or VPN.

    Does it work with on-premises Active Directory?

    Yes, with Pro, including from cloud-only laptops that aren't joined to the domain. EntraPlus connects to your domain controller over the network or VPN, detects accounts that sync from AD and saves their changes there.

    Does it work without on-premises Active Directory?

    Yes. Cloud-only schools get everything except the Active Directory features.

    Do I need to install anything else?

    Not for users, groups, devices or policies. Exchange and Active Directory features each need a free Microsoft module, and EntraPlus installs it for you with one click.

    Which versions of Windows does it run on?

    Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. It doesn't run on a Mac. Windows may warn you the first time you run the installer because it isn't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.

    Is it only for schools?

    It was built in a school, so the language and defaults suit schools and trusts: academic-year expiry dates, education licences, staff and student groups. It works the same for any organisation on Microsoft 365.
