Identity and users

One screen for cloud and hybrid accounts

From a new starter's first morning to the leaver checklist on their last day, each person is one screen.

  • Details, groups, licences and email addresses on one form. Accounts that sync from Active Directory are saved in AD.
  • New starters from one form, with the sign-in name built for you, or a whole September intake from a spreadsheet, with a preview before anything is created.
  • Reset passwords, unlock, sign someone out everywhere, or restore a deleted account.
  • The leaver (offboarding) in one go: block sign-in, sign out, reset the password, remove groups and licences, and convert the mailbox to shared.
  • Changes stay highlighted until you save them, and Undo changes puts them back.

Devices and Intune

BitLocker recovery keys and LAPS passwords in seconds

When someone's staring at the BitLocker recovery screen, you don't want to click through Intune. Type the device name, read out the key.

  • Every Intune device with its compliance, last check-in, encryption and free storage.
  • Ready-made filters: not compliant, not checked in for 14 or 30 days, not encrypted, personal devices.
  • Sync now, restart, retire or wipe. Retire and wipe are only in advanced mode, and wiping means typing the device name first.
  • BitLocker keys and LAPS passwords from Intune or Active Directory. Each time one is viewed, it's written to the activity log.

About 5 minutes saved per recovery key, by our estimate.

Active Directory and hybrid

Active Directory and hybrid accounts, without the server console

Browse your OU tree, see what's in each OU and move things with a drag. No remote desktop to a domain controller.

  • Drag a whole classroom of computers onto a new OU in one go.
  • Computers not seen for 90 days, ready to disable or delete, with a confirmation each time.
  • Unlock accounts, reset passwords, create new AD accounts and set an expiry date, with the end of the academic year one click away.
  • Deleted AD accounts can be restored, as long as the AD Recycle Bin is turned on.

Pro Active Directory needs Pro

About 15 minutes saved per classroom move and 4 per unlock, by our estimate.

Security and apps

Conditional Access and expiring app secrets, in plain English

Every app connected to your tenant, who publishes it and when its secrets expire. An expired secret is a common reason a sync suddenly stops.

  • Secrets and certificates that have expired or are about to, in one ready-made filter.
  • Spot unverified publishers, and switch an app's sign-in off, with a confirmation.
  • Conditional Access policies explained in plain English.
  • Policy templates start in report-only mode, with your own account excluded.

Find, report and export

A dashboard, saved filters and CSV export

Open EntraPlus in the morning and what needs you is already listed. Most of this works on the Free plan.

Safe for the whole team

Basic mode: safe for your newest technician

Give everyone the tools they need and nothing that could hurt on a bad day. Managers decide who gets what.

  • Basic mode hides deleting, wiping and the leaver. Advanced mode shows everything.
  • Managers choose each person's role on the EntraPlus website, so nobody can switch themselves up.
  • Deleting a user or group, or retiring or wiping a device, means typing its name first.
  • Every change goes in the activity log with who made it. Passwords and keys never do.
